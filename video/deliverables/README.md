# ClimbSmarter — five spots, cut and ready

All five are 1080×1920, 30fps, H.264, mastered to −14 LUFS (the level Instagram,
TikTok and YouTube normalise to, so none of them will get turned down on ingest).
Every one carries burned-in karaoke captions, because the default is muted.

Nothing here is a mockup. The app footage is your own screen recording with the
Safari chrome cropped off; the climbing is your own gym footage, tone-mapped off
the iPhone's HDR so it doesn't come out grey. The music is written for these
edits rather than licensed, so there are no rights attached to any of them.

---

## 1. It Adapts — 20s · paid

**The claim it proves:** the weekly check-in actually rewrites next week, and you
can watch it happen.

**Hook (0–2s):** climber pumped on the black-volume wall.
> MOST TRAINING PLANS / don't know you had a **bad week.**

**Beat sheet**

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | the attempt, hook text |
| 2–4 | check-in form, tapping **Not really / Weak** |
| 4–6 | all three answers selected |
| 6–9 | typing the note, live, on the real keyboard |
| 9–11 | the note finished, **SUBMIT & ADAPT PLAN** |
| 11–12 | *Check-in Saved — we're adjusting your plan* |
| 12–15 | the rebuilt session |
| 15–17.5 | the send |
| 17.5–20 | end card |

**Voiceover**
> This one asks how your week actually went. Not just whether you finished it —
> did it feel weak? You answer the rest in your own words. And next week gets
> rebuilt around it. It's not a PDF. It's paying attention.

**End card:** *It doesn't just track* / It asks. Then it rewrites the plan.

## 2. Ask It Anything — 20s · paid

**The claim it proves:** the coach gives real coaching, not a generic answer.
This is one genuine exchange, unedited.

**Hook (0–2s):** you walk straight past the lens.
> TWO YEARS OF GUESSING / So I just **asked it.**

**Beat sheet**

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | walk-past, hook text |
| 2–5 | the question sent, the coach thinking |
| 5–11 | the real reply, slow push in |
| 11–14 | macro: a foot placed silently on a hold |
| 14–17.5 | the session it built |
| 17.5–20 | end card |

**Voiceover**
> I asked it how to warm up for hangboard. Five rounds — cruise the first three
> with perfect form, then bump it a notch. And then this: on every move, exhale,
> and place your feet silently. If you hear a stomp, drop a grade.

That last line is quoted off your screen, not written for the ad. That's the
whole reason this one works.

**End card:** *A coach in the app* / Ask it anything. Any time.

## 3. The Fueling Plan — 18s · paid

**The claim it proves:** the app does nutrition too. This is the only spot
carrying *new information* rather than another training claim, which is why it
is the best cold-audience option of the four.

**Hook (0–2s):** you under the roof against the windows.
> NOBODY TALKS ABOUT / the other **half** of training.

**Beat sheet**

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | gym wide, hook text |
| 2–4 | logging the day's intake |
| 4–6 | the bars filling against the day's targets |
| 6–9 | AI Fueling Plan, breakfast, real macros |
| 9–11.5 | pre-session fuel, lunch, hydration |
| 11.5–13.5 | **REFRESH** → a whole new plan |
| 13.5–15.5 | back to the wall |
| 15.5–18 | end card |

**Voiceover**
> It doesn't just tell you what to climb. Log what you ate, and it tracks it
> against the day it planned for you. Protein oats. Banana and rice cakes an
> hour out. Don't like it? Regenerate it. One app. Both halves.

**End card:** *Training + nutrition* / One plan. Both halves.

## 4. The Grind — 22s · paid or organic

**The angle:** not a before/after — a *during*. No app UI at all until the last
card. This is the one that doesn't look like an app ad.

**Hook (0–2s):**
> TODAY'S SESSION / **Fail.** On purpose.

**Beat sheet:** eight attempts cut one per second across two problems, a macro
beat, then you walk past the lens as a natural wipe into the send at 12s, which
runs to the top.

**Voiceover**
> Board limit practice. Three problems, five attempts each. That's the whole
> session — try something just out of reach, on purpose. You're not meant to send
> it today. You're meant to get closer. And then one day, it goes.

**End card:** *Built around your project* / Stop guessing. Start sending.

## 5. Tip 01 — Quiet Feet — 20s · organic

Different job from the four above. This one isn't trying to convert on one view;
it teaches one thing and plugs softly at the end. Post it, don't boost it.

**Hook (0–2s):**
> TIP 01 / Loud feet = **wasted energy.**

**Beat sheet**

| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | macro foot on a hold, backlit |
| 2–8 | two clean placements, close |
| 8–12 | the explainer diagram: stomp vs. quiet |
| 12–15 | weighting the foot and driving off it |
| 15–18 | where the cue came from — the coach chat |
| 18–20 | soft end card |

**Voiceover**
> Don't stomp it. Look at the hold, and place the toe once. Shuffle or re-grip,
> and your fingers pay for it. A stomp bounces you off the hold; a quiet
> placement presses into it. Place it once. Then trust it. That cue is straight
> out of my coaching app.

**End card:** *Quiet feet · Tip 01* / Want more coaching like this?

**This is a template, not a one-off.** Same hook / demo / soft-CTA shape, new
subject each time — smearing, heel hooks, drop knees. Once you film a couple
more, each new tip is a few hours, not a day.

## Where to put them

- **Cold traffic:** 3 (Fueling Plan) first — it's the only one that tells people
  something they didn't know the app did. Then 1 (It Adapts).
- **Retargeting / people who've seen the app:** 2 (Ask It Anything). It's the
  proof-of-quality piece and it's wasted on a cold audience.
- **Organic feed:** 4 (The Grind) and 5 (Quiet Feet). Neither reads as an ad.
- **All five are vertical-safe:** captions sit clear of the Reels/TikTok caption
  and CTA furniture along the bottom, and nothing important is in the top 12%.

## Things worth fixing when you next record

  in the app history if you ever want that shot.
- Nothing in the footage shows a rest day or a deload. That's the one obvious
  ad angle the current material can't support.
